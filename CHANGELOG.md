# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Zoom and pan with the mouse wheel, a pinch or a drag (1x to 20x), and zoom in, zoom out and reset
  buttons. New spec field `zoom` (default true).
- Click a country to zoom to it; click it again, the sea or reset to zoom out. New spec field
  `clickToZoom` (default true). Small far-away islands are left out of the zoom box.
- Country names on the map from 2x zoom.
- City layer: series with `latitude` and `longitude` labels are drawn as points sized and coloured by
  value, named from 4x zoom. New spec fields `cityLabel`, `latitudeLabel`, `longitudeLabel` and
  `showCities` (`auto`, `always`, `never`; default `auto`). Points with invalid coordinates are listed
  under the map.
- The view survives data refreshes and theme switches.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added

- `GeoMapChart` panel: a world choropleth shaded by country from any time-series query that carries
  an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 country label (configurable, default `country`).
- Per-series reduction with any Perses calculation; series with the same country are summed.
- Sequential colour schemes (blues, greens, oranges, purples, reds, viridis) with light and dark
  ramps, linear or log scale, optional min and max, and a continuous legend.
- Country names in tooltips as `Name (CODE)`, from English CLDR names baked into the map data, with
  `XX` (Unknown) and `T1` (Tor) handled, and an optional `country_name` series label as fallback.
- Codes without a shape are listed under the map instead of being dropped.
- Panel settings editor and CUE schema.
- Natural Earth 1:50m map data, generated by `npm run world-data` and bundled (no runtime fetches).
- `Containerfile` for a plugin-only OCI image, for use as a Kubernetes image volume.

Tested with Perses v0.54.0.
